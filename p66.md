### System1 (Bently Nevada)

**Machinery Condition Monitoring**

Tracks real-time health data (vibration, temperature, pressure) of heavy rotating equipment like turbines, pumps, and compressors to predict mechanical failures.

### PCMS (MISTRAS Group)

**Plant Condition Management System**

Serves as the central database for piping/vessel corrosion monitoring, thickness tracking (TMLs), Risk-Based Inspection (RBI), and regulatory compliance (API/OSHA).

### Toadfly IPS

**Integrated Planning & Scheduling**

Manages large-scale refinery turnarounds, outages, and plant shutdowns (TA/STOs), covering scope development, labor costs, and real-time field progress tracking.

### CNotes

Digital Shift Handover & Operations Log

Replaces paper operator logbooks to facilitate structured shift transitions, tracking process anomalies, safety bypasses, and daily operational logs.

### Toadfly ECR (Engineering Change Request)

Management of Change (MOC) Workflow

Standardizes internal requests to modify physical plant assets, piping, or operational procedures to enforce safety compliance before executing physical changes.

### AutoCAD / Autodesk

**2D/3D CAD Engineering & Modeling**

Used by plant engineers to design, draft, and modify technical plant schematics, piping designs, and facility layouts.

### Bentley View

**CAD File Viewer & Redlining**

A lightweight viewer allowing field technicians, maintenance managers, and non-engineers to view, measure, and markup DGN and DWG drawing files without requiring a full CAD license.

### Guideware

**Inspection Data Gathering & Field Analytics**

Captures field inspection data directly on mobile/handheld devices and syncs thickness logs, equipment checklists, and corrosion reports back into primary asset integrity databases (like PCMS).


#### How They Interconnect in Your Ecosystem

As an application manager, it helps to view these systems by their operational groupings:

Asset Integrity & Reliability: System1 monitors real-time rotating machinery health, PCMS tracks static equipment corrosion/safety compliance, and Guideware collects the field data to feed those integrity databases.

Plant Operations & Turnarounds: CNotes ensures clean communication between daily operator shifts, Toadfly IPS coordinates major plant turnaround projects, and ECR manages the engineering sign-offs for facility changes.

Engineering & Facilities: Autodesk creates/edits plant schematics, while Bentley View provides read-only drawing access across site teams.
